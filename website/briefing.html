CYVL institutional briefing

A concise overview of CYVL for partners, researchers and policy professionals. This page is designed for printing or saving as a PDF.

Mission & vision

Mission: Reduce conflict and improve stability in co‑parenting by turning conversations, events and schedules into clear, structured data.

Vision: Build systems that promote fairness, transparency and stability for families through neutral infrastructure.

Framework overview

CYVL operates through six layers designed to capture, organise and present information without bias:

  1. Structured communication — A guided environment for civil, on‑topic messaging.
  2. Guided event logging — Predefined categories for recording events objectively.
  3. Schedule stability tracking — Records planned schedules and deviations to expose reliability patterns.
  4. Pattern visualisation — Converts data into visual insights such as timelines and charts.
  5. Contextual resource pop‑ups — Offers neutral resources at key moments to support users.
  6. Courtroom artifact reporting — Generates professional, neutral reports on demand for institutional use.

Safeguards & neutrality

CYVL prioritises neutrality and data privacy. Users control when reports are generated and what data is included. Harassment and misuse are discouraged through platform design and policies.
