Privacy policy

CYVL respects your privacy. This page outlines what data we collect and how it is used. This document is provided as a starting point and does not constitute legal advice.

Information we collect

We may collect basic contact information that you provide through our forms, such as your name, email address and message. We also collect usage data about how you interact with the CYVL website to improve our services.

How we use information

Information you provide is used only to respond to enquiries, send updates and improve our services. Usage data is anonymised and aggregated wherever possible.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared with service providers that assist us in operating the website, but only under strict confidentiality obligations.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, please email us at info@cyvl.org.
