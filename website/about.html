About CYVL

CYVL exists to engineer stability in co‑parenting systems. Our work is grounded in neutrality, structure and clarity.

Mission

Our mission is to reduce conflict and improve stability in co‑parenting arrangements by providing neutral, structured systems that turn conversations and events into clear, organised data.

Vision

We envision a world where co‑parenting interactions are guided by systems that promote fairness, transparency and stability—supporting the well‑being of children and families.

Principles

  • Neutrality: CYVL does not take sides. All records and reports are strictly unbiased.
  • Structure: Data is captured through clear categories and processes rather than free-form narratives.
  • Clarity: We prioritise clear, visual communication over complexity.
  • Pattern Recognition: Insights come from trends and patterns—not isolated events or opinions.
  • Stability Engineering: We believe stability is designed through systems rather than negotiated under stress.
  • Privacy & Safety: Users control what they share. Data ownership is transparent. No harassment is tolerated.
